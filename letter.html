To My Favorite Person
Happy Birthday!

Dear Mima,

You once told me you were afraid of being a bad person, but honestly, I’ve never seen that in you. To me, you’re one of the kindest people I know—so kind that sometimes I worry you’ll end up blaming yourself for things that aren't your fault. My only wish is for you to rely on me more, whenever you're tired, sad, or feeling lost.

I love it when you call me sayang, kakak, and pacar. I love listening to you share things with so much excitement, and I love seeing you happy. Please don't carry your sadness alone. Keep smiling, and whenever things feel heavy, remember you can always talk to me.

Happy birthday!! Janji aku gakan nakal dikit (5%-an lah). 💗

With all my love,
Dicka 🌧️
